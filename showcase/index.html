What can I expect after I apply?

After you apply for a job at Bold Agency, you can expect to receive a call from one of our friendly and knowledgeable recruitment specialists. They will ask you questions about your experience and skillset, and will match you with the perfect job for your qualifications. You can also expect to receive guidance and support throughout the application process, as well as career advice and tips from our team of experts.

What does the selection process look like?

We have a multi-step selection process that is designed to identify the best candidates for our open positions. First, we accept applications and resumes online or via email. We also encourage potential candidates to include a cover letter and contact information so that we can follow up with them. Next, our team of recruiters reviews all of the applications and identifies the most qualified candidates. These candidates are then invited to participate in a phone screen, which is an opportunity for us to learn more about their skills and experience. Finally, the top candidates are invited to come in for an interview with Bold Agency's hiring manager. This is usually the last step in the process, and it's an opportunity for us to get to know the candidate better and ask any remaining questions we may have.

What is different about Bold Agency?

At Bold Agency, we pride ourselves on being different. We don't believe in the traditional agency model. We're a team of creative thinkers, doers, and problem-solvers who are passionate about promoting healthy life and helping our clients succeed. We're nimble, adaptable, and always looking for new and better ways to do things. We're also a lot of fun to work with!

Will I be allowed to work remotely from another city/country?

Yes, you will be able to work remotely. We value the ability to be flexible and offer remote work as an option for our employees.
